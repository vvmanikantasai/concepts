# Listening and Active Communication


### What are the steps/strategies to do Active Listening? 

* Do not think about your reply while the other person is talking  
* Focus fully on the speaker and what they are saying  
* Do not interrupt, let them finish properly  
* Show you are listening by nodding or eye contact  
* Say small things like "ok", "I see", "tell me more"  
* Ask simple questions if you don’t understand  
* Repeat what they said in your own words to check  

---

## According to Fisher's model, what are the key points of Reflective Listening? 

* Listen carefully to what the person is saying  
* Also notice their feelings, not just words  
* Show that you understand how they feel  
* Repeat their point in simple words  
* Ask them if you understood correctly  
* Try to build trust by listening properly  

---

### What are the obstacles in your listening process?

* I get distracted easily by phone or noise  
* Sometimes I think about something else while listening  
* I assume things before the person finishes  
* I interrupt because I feel I already understood  

---

### What can you do to improve your listening?

* Keep my phone away while talking  
* Let the other person finish fully  
* Focus more and not think about reply early  
* Repeat what they said to make sure I understood  

---

### When do you switch to Passive communication style in your day to day life?

* When I don’t want to argue with someone  
* When I feel shy or not confident  
* When I think my opinion will not matter  
* When I don’t want to hurt others  

---

### When do you switch into Aggressive communication styles in your day to day life?

* When I get very angry or frustrated  
* When I feel someone is not respecting me  
* When I lose patience  

---

### When do you switch into Passive Aggressive (sarcasm/gossiping/taunts/silent treatment and others) communication styles in your day to day life?

* When I am upset but don’t say it directly  
* When I use sarcasm instead of being clear  
* When I ignore someone instead of talking  
* When I feel ignored but don’t speak openly  

---

### How can you make your communication assertive? 

* Try to understand my feelings clearly  
* Know what I actually need  
* Speak up in small situations first  
* Use simple and clear words  
* Speak early instead of waiting and getting angry  
* Stay calm and not sound rude
